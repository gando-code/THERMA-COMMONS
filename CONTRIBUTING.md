# Contribution guide

Discuss scope before making major changes. Clearly label simulated and measured evidence. Include tests for changed control or validation behavior and preserve raw data provenance. Do not contribute confidential employer or institutional materials, personal records or unreviewed hardware-operating instructions.

Document actual authorship and technical contributions. Contributions are not automatically licensed for commercial use by this repository; agree on suitable rights before acceptance. Do not assume a sponsorship payment establishes ownership.

Use a branch and reviewable pull request. Include the problem, resulting behavior, test command and limits. Do not claim improved food safety, nutrition, accuracy or energy performance without suitable evidence.
