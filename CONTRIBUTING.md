# Contribution guide

Discuss scope before major changes. Clearly label simulated and measured evidence. Include tests for changed control or validation behavior and preserve raw data provenance. Do not contribute confidential employer or institutional materials, personal records or unreviewed hardware-operating instructions.

## License of contributions

By intentionally submitting a contribution for inclusion in the MPL-covered software identified in RIGHTS.md, you agree to license that contribution under MPL-2.0 and confirm that you have authority to do so. Retain accurate copyright notices and apply the Exhibit A notice or SPDX identifier to new covered source files. No copyright assignment or contributor license agreement is implied.

Contributions to reserved documents, example data or proposal-generation materials require explicit written agreement on use and distribution before acceptance. Do not infer a transfer of ownership from sponsorship or payment. Record actual authorship and technical contributions. Relicensing a contribution outside MPL requires rights from its holder where applicable; this policy does not grant those rights automatically.

Use a branch and reviewable pull request. Include the problem, resulting behavior, test command and limits. Do not claim improved food safety, nutrition, accuracy or energy performance without suitable evidence.
