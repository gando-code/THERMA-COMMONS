# Roadmap and acceptance gates

## Delivered in this repository

15 proposal documents; rendered PDFs; budget model; research controller; synthetic water simulation; independent-reference analyzer; format fixture; tests; CI; development, safety, IP and hardware planning guides.

## Next phase

1. Appoint engineering and measurement reviewers; verify applicant and funding terms.
2. Review architecture, quote components and freeze the operating envelope.
3. Build and commission a supervised non-food bench rig with independent protections.
4. Develop controls on separate tuning data.
5. Run the frozen 60-trial campaign and comparator workflow.
6. Review failures, uncertainty, cleanability, cost and customer evidence.
7. Decide whether to stop, redesign or fund the next application.

## Explicitly deferred

Physical hardware drivers, embedded safety firmware, PCB/CAD fabrication files, RF integration, food-specific process validation, certification, production tooling, app/cloud services, royalty agreements and actual sponsor submissions. These cannot be completed truthfully from a concept alone. Deliverables and owners should be agreed before each phase.
